Our Story · 월화

From Seoul’s butchers to Manila’s grills

Wolhwa Group has spent more than 16 years in one of Korea’s most competitive food scenes — and earned a place in the prestigious Blue Ribbon Survey five years in a row.

Our CEO, Junhyeong Jeon, holds the title of Master Butcher of Korea, and we hold patents for the dry-aging process behind our signature samgyupsal. It’s why the menu stays focused: nine carefully selected cuts, marinated in the signature sauce Korea already loves, cooked tender and juicy at your table.
